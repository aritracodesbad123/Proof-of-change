# The swarm-on arm is analysed on 7 of the 10 drawn pairs

## What changed

Nothing in the code. This entry records how the exploratory swarm-on arm is counted.

## Facts

- 10 swarm-on pairs were drawn from the 92-pair study list on the study-v1 tag, with seed 20261020, as set out in
  A1 section 12 and in the 2026-10-20 swarm-pair-draw entry.
- 3 of those 10 pairs cannot be installed.
- The swarm-on arm is therefore analysed on the remaining 7 pairs. No replacement pairs were drawn.

## Why

Drawing replacements after the draw would change a choice that had to be fixed before the study ran, and the
draw would no longer repeat from its seed. Keeping the original 10 and reporting 7 analysed keeps the draw as
registered.

## What it means for you

Write the swarm arm as "10 drawn, 7 analysed". The draw itself, and the 2026-10-20 swarm-pair-draw entry, stay
at 10.
