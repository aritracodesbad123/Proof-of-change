# An observation belongs to the most specific claim that covers it

An observation covered by several claims was always given to the first one. A broad "same" claim listed first (covering a, b and c) took an observation that a later claim names on its own as a difference, so a proven difference read as a contradiction.

Now the most specific claim wins: a cover equal to the name beats a wildcard match, then the claim with fewer cover patterns, then the first claim. An observation covered by only one claim is unchanged, and a difference that no claim covers is still unclaimed, so no uncovered difference gets quieter. A contradiction against the claim the observation now belongs to still needs attention.
