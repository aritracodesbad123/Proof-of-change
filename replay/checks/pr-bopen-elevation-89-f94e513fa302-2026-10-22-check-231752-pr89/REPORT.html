Glossary

TermMeaning
mainThe library as it is today, without the PR.
PRPull request: a proposed change to the library.
runOne full pass of the selected tests. We do 3 per side, alternating main and PR.
flakyA test that gives different results when nothing changed; it is ignored as evidence.
log lineA line in a pytest-*.log file: the raw output the verdict is based on.
routeHow the two sides were built: nightly+patch, source-build or source-pair (see docs/OPERATIONS.md).
changed lines coveredHow many of the PR's changed, executable Python lines ran during the PR side's tests.
re-parametrizedA test whose parameter ids changed (renamed cases); the new ids are judged on their own.
